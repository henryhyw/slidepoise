# Sample production

The current [Consulting and Editorial samples](../examples/) use the same packaged reconstruction path. Their selected designs, semantic maps and reconstruction handoffs are retained with the final PowerPoint files.

Consulting uses a generated content area with inherited headers and footers. Editorial preserves photographic collage and native text. Its third page uses three separately regenerated transparent illustrations. Cross-page reviews record the shared title and folio treatments.

Use the [rebuild command](../examples/README.md#rebuild-a-sample) to reproduce construction. The samples were reviewed with LibreOffice on macOS. Their typography uses Arial and Georgia, with Courier New in the Editorial design. Other fonts or renderers may change wrapping.

Earlier calibration attempts, production plans and dated status reports are available in Git history. They are not part of the current build or installation.
