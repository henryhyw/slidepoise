# Browser review

This capture set documents the product page in the Codex in-app browser. The source hashes, viewport measurements and actual interaction results are recorded in [browser-review.json](browser-review.json).

- [chart-reconstruction.png](chart-reconstruction.png) shows the three whole-card views of the same chart, from its interpretation and pixel measurements to its editable PowerPoint object.
- [presentation-plan.png](presentation-plan.png) shows the presentation plan with page navigation and substantive content, including real metrics, tables and decision logic.
- [mobile-plan.png](mobile-plan.png) shows the mobile plan, where selecting a page keeps the dialog open and its full content readable. Wide tables scroll within their own region.
- [viewer-alignment.png](viewer-alignment.png) shows the shared boundary beneath the thumbnail rail and slide canvas. The caption spans the viewer, and the download sits beside the presentation description.
- [mobile-samples.png](mobile-samples.png) shows the visible sample previews and mobile presentation controls.
- [mobile-workflow.png](mobile-workflow.png) shows the four shared stages with aligned previews and named evidence disclosures.

The review also covers the complete planning content of all five consulting pages and the editorial example, initial and revision input disclosures, historical editorial inputs, native chart selection, nested dialogs, comparison endpoints, content-frame alignment and keyboard focus restoration. Ordinary content cards use their whole surface for interaction. The studio retains its single global expansion control.

Earlier screenshots and superseded review records remain in the history directories under `output/product-qa-v4` at the repository root. They are separate from this capture set.
